# Recipe Scaler - Quick Start Guide for New Features

## 🚀 Getting Started

All features are **automatically enabled** when you use the application. No setup required!

---

## Feature Quick Reference

### 1. 🥘 Ingredient Categorization
**What it does**: Automatically organizes ingredients into categories
- Main Ingredients
- Spices & Seasonings  
- Liquids
- Proteins
- Garnish & Toppings

**How to use**: 
- Just fetch a recipe from YouTube or enter manually
- Ingredients are automatically categorized and displayed with emoji indicators

---

### 2. 🔄 Unit Conversion Toggle
**What it does**: Switch recipe units between Original, Metric, and Imperial

**How to use**:
1. Go to "Scaled Recipe" page
2. Look for "Unit System" dropdown at the top
3. Select:
   - **Original Units** - Keep as-is
   - **Metric (g, ml)** - Convert to grams/milliliters
   - **Imperial (oz, cups)** - Convert to ounces/cups

**Supported Units**:
- Weight: gram, kg, oz, lb
- Volume: ml, liter, cup, tbsp, tsp, oz

---

### 3. ✏️ Editable Scaled Output
**What it does**: Let you adjust ingredient quantities after scaling

**How to use**:
1. On the "Scaled Recipe" page, you'll see a hint: "💡 Tip: Click on any ingredient to edit"
2. **Click on any ingredient** to open edit mode
3. **Type the new quantity** (e.g., change "1 cup" to "3/4 cup")
4. **Press Enter** or click elsewhere to save
5. Your edits are automatically saved and included in exports

**Features**:
- Edit individual ingredients
- Changes persist through unit conversion
- Included in PDF, text, and print outputs
- Easily revert by refreshing the page

---

### 4. 🔍 Duplicate Ingredient Detection
**What it does**: Automatically detects and merges duplicate ingredients

**How it works**:
- "onion" + "onions" → merged as one
- "garlic clove" + "garlic cloves" → quantities combined
- "salt" + "salt" → added together

**Examples of merging**:
| Input | Result |
|-------|--------|
| 2 cups flour + 1 cup flour | 3 cups flour |
| 1 tsp salt + 1/2 tsp salt | 1.5 tsp salt |
| "olive oil" + "oil" | Both kept (different specificity) |

**Automatic**: This happens behind the scenes during scaling!

---

### 5. 📝 Recipe Notes & Instructions
**What it does**: Add cooking notes and step-by-step instructions

**How to use**:

#### On "Enter Recipe Manually" Page:
1. Scroll down to "Cooking Notes & Instructions" section
2. **Add Notes**: Type any cooking tips in the "Cooking Notes" textarea
3. **Add Steps**: Click "+ Add Step" for each instruction
4. **Edit/Remove**: Modify steps or click × to remove

#### On "Scaled Recipe" Page:
1. Your notes and steps appear below the ingredients
2. Can edit quantities of ingredients
3. All information is preserved

#### When Exporting:
- **PDF**: Notes and instructions formatted nicely
- **Text File**: Includes all information
- **Print**: Everything prints properly
- **Email**: All content included
- **Save**: Recipe saved with notes and steps

**Example Format**:
```
Chicken Pasta

Ingredients:
- 2 cups pasta
- 1 lb chicken breast
- 2 cups sauce

Cooking Notes:
- Preheat oven to 350°F
- Cook pasta al dente (8-10 minutes)

Instructions:
1. Dice chicken into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook chicken until golden brown
4. Add sauce and simmer for 10 minutes
5. Toss with cooked pasta
```

## FAQ

**Q: Will my edits be saved permanently?**
A: Edits are saved in the current session. Use "Save Recipe" to store permanently.

**Q: Can I undo my ingredient edits?**
A: Refresh the page to revert, or click unit system dropdown to reset.

**Q: Does unit conversion affect the recipe?**
A: No! It's just a display change. Original recipe is always preserved.

**Q: Can I edit notes after scaling?**
A: Yes! Add/edit notes on the scaled page and they'll be included in exports.

**Q: Will duplicates always merge?**
A: Almost always! The system uses smart name matching but keeps dissimilar items separate.

**Q: Can I print with all the new features?**
A: Yes! Categories, edits, notes, and instructions all print properly.

**Q: Do my saved recipes include notes?**
A: Yes! Saved recipes store everything - ingredients, notes, and instructions.

## Common Scenarios
